# AI Trading System

## Vision

Build a professional automated AI-assisted trading platform capable of trading:

- Cryptocurrency
- Forex
- Gold
- Stocks

## Phase 1
- Binance connection
- Historical data
- Backtesting
- Paper Trading
- Telegram notifications

## Phase 2
- Dashboard
- AI market analysis
- Performance reports

## Phase 3
- Live trading
- Portfolio management

## Risk Rules

- Risk 1% per trade
- Stop Loss mandatory
- No averaging down
- Daily maximum loss limit
- Every trade logged

## Goal

Only trade real money after successful testing.
